# Nimbus Notes: Refund Policy (fictional sample document)

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Cancelling stops the next renewal, but the
current month is not refunded.

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, annual
plans are refunded pro rata for the remaining full months, minus a processing fee of 15 USD.

Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 7 business days. Customers
who paid by bank transfer must provide their account holder name to the billing team.

Education accounts are free and therefore never refunded.
