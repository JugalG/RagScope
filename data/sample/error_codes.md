# Nimbus Notes: Sync Error Codes (fictional sample document)

ERR-4012: the device clock differs from the server clock by more than five minutes.
Fix: turn on automatic date and time in the device settings, then sync again.

ERR-5003: the notebook is larger than 2 GB, the maximum size for a single notebook.
Fix: split the notebook into two smaller notebooks.

ERR-7720: the account was signed in on more than 5 devices at once.
Fix: sign out of an unused device from the Account > Devices page.
