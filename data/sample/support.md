# Nimbus Notes: Support and Service Levels (fictional sample document)

Support is available by email on all plans. Business plans also get live chat between
9:00 and 18:00 IST, Monday to Friday.

Response time targets: Free plan 3 business days; Pro plan 1 business day; Business plan
4 hours for urgent tickets.

The service uptime target is 99.9% per calendar month for Business plans. If uptime falls
below the target, Business customers receive a 10% credit on the next invoice.
